# TrackerLens privacy policy

TrackerLens analyzes the apps installed on your device **entirely on your device**.

- **What it reads:** the list of installed apps, each app's name, version, requested permissions
  and its APK files, to detect embedded tracker code.
- **What it stores:** scan results, update history and your settings (including which apps you
  marked as reviewed) in the app's private storage on your device.
  They are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
- **What it shares:** nothing. TrackerLens has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
  "Share my results" only opens Android's share sheet with text you can review before sending.
- **Notifications:** alerts name the app and what its update added. On the lock screen only a
  generic "An app update reduced your privacy" is shown, and alerts are not sent to paired watches.
- **Deleting data:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/payallenka/trackerlens/issues
